# Sales Performance Analysis

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance across customer segments, products, and months, built to support management decisions around revenue distribution.

## Goal

Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance, helping management understand how revenue is distributed across customers, products, and months, and make decisions based on accurate data.

## Data

Detailed internal sales data including: sale date, product, customer segment, sales value, and profit — processed in Power Query and analyzed in Power BI Desktop over a twelve-month period.

## Dashboard (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** 119M
- **Total Profit:** 16.89M
- **Profit Margin:** 14%

## Key Results

**By customer segment**
- The Government segment is the largest contributor at 44% of sales (53M)
- Small Business is second at 36% (42M)
- Together, these two segments represent roughly 80% of total revenue

**By product**
- *Paseo* is the top-selling product by a clear margin (33.0M)
- Followed by *VTT* at 20.5M, while the remaining products range between 13.8M and 18.3M

**Monthly trend**
- Relatively low, stable sales from January through August
- A strong increase starting in September, peaking in October
- A clear seasonal pattern concentrated in the last quarter of the year

**Financial indicators**
- Total sales of 119M against total profit of 16.89M
- An overall profit margin of 14%, a key benchmark for future profitability comparisons

## Tools

`Power BI` · `Power Query` · `DAX`
